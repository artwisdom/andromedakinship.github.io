Need help with any Andromeda Kinship app? We're here to help.

Common Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, find the app's subscription, and tap Cancel Subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?
Go to the upgrade or subscription screen in the app and tap "Restore Purchases."

Where is my data stored?
Our apps store your data on your device. You can export or delete it anytime from the app's Settings screen.

How do I delete my data?
Go to Settings in the app and use the "Clear All Data" (or "Delete All Data") option.
