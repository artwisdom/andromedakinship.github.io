Your data

Your loan files, partners, comp plan, and ledger live on your device — the app works fully offline, and Fundline never asks for a borrower's Social Security number, credit score, account numbers, or contact details. You can clear everything from Settings → Clear Data, or by deleting the app.

Subscriptions

FUNDED PRO is managed through your Apple ID (Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions), where you can change or cancel it at any time. Purchases restore from the paywall's Restore purchases link.

A note on what Fundline is

Fundline is a personal record-keeping tool for loan originators. It is not a pricing engine, a loan origination system, or a compliance product, and it does not provide legal, tax, or financial advice. Your compensation plan, and whether any early-payoff provision applies to you, are governed by your own agreement with your employer or investor.
