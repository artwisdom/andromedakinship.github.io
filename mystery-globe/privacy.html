Privacy Policy — Redstring: Ancient Mystery Map

Last Updated: November 25, 2026 | Effective Date: November 25, 2026

Andromeda Kinship LLC ("Andromeda Kinship," "we," "our," or "us") operates the iOS application Redstring: Ancient Mystery Map (the "App"), a 3D globe pinned with 305 real locations tied to unexplained history and mysteries. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App does and does not handle, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

By using Redstring, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

The short version: There is no account and no login. The App shows no ads, contains no advertising SDK, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, health, or financial account information, and we do not sell your data. If you buy Full Clearance, RevenueCat manages your purchase under an anonymous ID, along with a few anonymous counts of how the upgrade screen is used. Narrated tapes and evidence images are downloaded from our own content server.

1. No Account, No Sign-Up

Redstring does not require an account, login, registration, or sign-up. The entire App is usable immediately when you open it. We do not create a user profile for you, and we do not ask you for credentials of any kind. Your in-app activity (which files you have opened, your rank and Field Keys, the files you have unsealed, your premium status, and app settings) is stored locally on your device.

2. Information We Collect

This section describes everything the App and the services built into it handle.

2.1 Purchases (RevenueCat)

If you buy Full Clearance, your purchase is processed by Apple and managed through RevenueCat so we can unlock and restore your premium entitlement. RevenueCat identifies your copy of the App with an anonymous, randomly generated ID, not with your name or Apple ID. Purchase history is used only to manage that entitlement. It is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you. We never receive or store your payment card, bank, or other financial account details — those are handled entirely by Apple.

2.2 Upgrade-Screen Usage (Product Interaction)

So we can tell which parts of the App lead people to the upgrade screen, the App stores a few anonymous values with RevenueCat under the same anonymous ID: where the upgrade screen was last opened from, how many times it has been opened, the date it was last opened, how many Field Keys you have used, the date you first used one, and the date of your first visit to the upgrade screen after that. These values contain no personal information, are used only for analytics and to run the App, are not linked to your identity, and are not used to track you.

2.3 Diagnostics

If you have chosen in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with crash reports and performance data for the App. We use these only to keep the App stable and improve it. This data is not linked to your identity.

2.4 Tapes and Images Downloaded from Our Content Server

The App downloads narrated tapes and evidence images from our content server at tapes.andromedakinship.com when you open them, and keeps them on your device so they play without a connection. As with any request to a server, the request necessarily includes your device's network (IP) connection information so the content can be returned. No name, account, or identifier is sent; the request is used only to deliver the content. Each case file also links to outside sources, such as Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons, which open only if you tap them and are then governed by those sites' own policies.

2.5 Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App schedules its reminders (Tonight's file, and a reminder before a free trial ends) on your device. No push-notification server is used and nothing about these reminders leaves your device.

2.6 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Contacts
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA) or advertising data
  • Health or fitness data
  • Financial account information (payment cards, bank accounts)

3. No Advertising and No Tracking

Starting with version 1.8, Redstring shows no ads and contains no advertising SDK. The App does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, does not use your device's advertising identifier, and does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission. Versions before 1.8 included Google's AdMob advertising SDK; updating to the latest version removes it.

4. Premium Purchases ("Full Clearance")

Redstring offers an optional premium tier called Full Clearance, sold as an Apple in-app purchase and managed with RevenueCat. Full Clearance unseals every sealed Full Case File with its narrated record, opens every red string (Thread) end to end, and adds the gold-foil Board export and the night-lights globe. All 305 case files, their photographs, and their teaser tapes stay free. Ranks, Field Keys, and the Vault are earned by using the App and are never sold.

4.2 Apple Auto-Renew Terms (Subscriptions)

The Monthly and Annual options are auto-renewable subscriptions. Standard Apple terms apply:

  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase.
  • A subscription automatically renews unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period.
  • You can manage your subscription and turn off auto-renew at any time in your App Store Account Settings after purchase.
  • The Annual plan's 7-day free trial is available once per Apple ID, as determined by Apple. If you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, you are not charged. Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited when you purchase a subscription.

The Forever option is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. It does not renew and is not a subscription.

6. Your Choices

  • Manage subscriptions: Turn off auto-renew or cancel in your App Store Account Settings.
  • Notifications: Turn the App's reminders on or off at any time in your device's Settings.
  • Diagnostics: Choose whether to share analytics with app developers in your device's Privacy settings.
  • Little to delete: Because there is no account and no login, we do not hold a personal profile tied to your identity that we could delete on request. Uninstalling the App removes the data stored locally on your device. To ask about the anonymous purchase and upgrade-screen values held by RevenueCat, contact us at the address below.

7. Data Retention

  • On-device data: Your premium status, opened files, rank, Field Keys, unsealed files, downloaded tapes and images, and settings are stored locally and remain until you delete them or uninstall the App.
  • Purchase records and upgrade-screen values: Retained by Apple and RevenueCat as needed to manage and restore your entitlement, in accordance with their policies.
  • Diagnostics: Retained by Apple in accordance with its policies.
  • We do not maintain a separate server-side database of personal user profiles for this App.

8. Data Is Not Sold

We do not sell your personal information. Data shared with third parties is limited to what is described in this policy and is used to process purchases, provide diagnostics, and deliver the App's content.

9. Children and Age

Redstring is rated 12+ and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App contains general-interest history and mystery content. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has used the App in a way that raises a concern, please contact us at support@andromedakinship.com.

10. Nature of the Content

Redstring is an entertainment and general-interest education app. Case files carry an honest evidence badge — DOCUMENTED, DEBATED, or FRINGE — and links to their sources. "FRINGE" items are clearly labeled and presented as claims alongside the mainstream or official counter-position. The App does not assert paranormal claims as fact and does not provide professional, medical, legal, or financial advice.
