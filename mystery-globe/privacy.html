Privacy Policy — Redstring: Ancient Mystery Map

Last Updated: July 12, 2026 | Effective Date: July 12, 2026

Andromeda Kinship LLC ("Andromeda Kinship," "we," "our," or "us") operates the iOS application Redstring: Ancient Mystery Map (the "App"), a premium 3D globe pinned with 305 real locations tied to unexplained history and mysteries. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App does and does not handle, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

By using Redstring, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

The short version: There is no account and no login. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, precise location, health, or financial account information. We do not sell your data. The main data flow is advertising: the App shows Google AdMob ads, and — only if you allow tracking through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt — an advertising identifier may be used to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

1. No Account, No Sign-Up

Redstring does not require an account, login, registration, or sign-up. The entire App is usable immediately when you open it. We do not create a user profile for you, and we do not ask you for credentials of any kind. Your in-app activity (which pins you have opened, your premium status, and app settings) is stored locally on your device.

2. Information We Collect

The categories below match the App's App Store privacy label.

2.1 Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. In connection with advertising, the following may be collected:

  • Device identifier for advertising (IDFA): collected only if you allow tracking when Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt appears. If you do not allow tracking, the IDFA is not used.
  • Advertising data: information about the ads shown to you (for example, which ads were served or interacted with).
  • Product interaction / usage data: in-app events used to deliver, measure, and improve advertising and app functionality.

This data is used for third-party advertising, analytics, and app functionality. This data may be used to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. In App Store privacy terms, these fall under the categories Identifiers and Usage Data.

2.2 Purchases

If you buy the premium tier ("Clearance"), your purchase is processed by Apple and managed through RevenueCat so we can unlock and restore your premium entitlement. Purchase history is used only to manage that entitlement. It is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you. We never receive or store your payment card, bank, or other financial account details — those are handled entirely by Apple.

2.3 Diagnostics

We collect crash data, performance data, and other diagnostic data to keep the App stable and improve it. This diagnostic data is used for analytics and app functionality and is not linked to your identity.

2.4 Third-Party Content Fetched at Runtime (Wikipedia)

Each case file links to a Wikipedia source. To show source imagery and summaries, the App fetches thumbnails and summary text from the Wikipedia REST API at the time you view content. As with any request to a third-party server, the request necessarily includes your device's network (IP) connection information so the content can be returned. We do not receive a name or account from this process; it is used only to display the requested source material.

2.5 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Contacts
  • Precise location or geolocation
  • Health or fitness data
  • Financial account information (payment cards, bank accounts)

3. Advertising, IDFA, and App Tracking Transparency

Redstring is free to use and supported by occasional advertising served through Google AdMob. On iOS, apps must ask for your permission before tracking you across other companies' apps and websites. Redstring uses Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework to request that permission.

  • If you allow tracking, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to personalize ads and measure advertising across other apps and websites. In this case, your data may be used to track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • If you do not allow tracking (or later turn it off), the IDFA is not used to track you across other apps. You may still see ads, but they are not linked to you through the cross-app advertising identifier.

3.1 How Ads Are Shown

Ads in Redstring are limited and designed to stay out of your way:

  • Ads are interstitial ads shown only after you close a case file — never over the map, a pin, or content you are reading.
  • Ad frequency is capped, so you will not see an ad every time.
  • No ads are shown during your first session.
  • The premium tier removes ads entirely (see Section 4).

6. Your Choices

  • Opt out of tracking (ATT): Decline the App Tracking Transparency prompt, or change it any time under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your device.
  • Limit ad tracking: You can turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" and reset or restrict your advertising identifier in iOS Settings.
  • Remove ads: Purchase the Clearance premium tier for an ad-free experience.
  • Manage subscriptions: Turn off auto-renew or cancel in your App Store Account Settings.
  • Little to delete: Because there is no account and no login, we do not hold a personal profile tied to your identity that we could delete on request. Uninstalling the App removes the data stored locally on your device.

7. Data Retention

  • On-device data: Your premium status, viewed-pin state, and settings are stored locally and remain until you delete them or uninstall the App.
  • Advertising and diagnostic data: Retained by the relevant third-party processors (Google AdMob for advertising; Apple's diagnostics pipeline) in accordance with their own policies and retention practices.
  • Purchase records: Retained by Apple and RevenueCat as needed to manage and restore your entitlement.
  • We do not maintain a separate server-side database of personal user profiles for this App.

8. Data Is Not Sold

We do not sell your personal information. Data shared with third parties is limited to what is described in this policy and is used to operate advertising, process purchases, provide diagnostics, and display third-party source content.

9. Children and Age

Redstring is rated 12+ and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App contains general-interest history and mystery content and displays advertising. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has used the App in a way that raises a concern, please contact us at support@andromedakinship.com.

10. Nature of the Content

Redstring is an entertainment and general-interest education app. Case files carry an honest evidence badge — DOCUMENTED, DEBATED, or FRINGE — and a Wikipedia source link. "FRINGE" items are clearly labeled and presented as claims alongside the mainstream or official counter-position. The App does not assert paranormal claims as fact and does not provide professional, medical, legal, or financial advice.
