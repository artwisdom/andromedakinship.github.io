The lasting asset is the feedback loop: a failure becomes a written learning, then a candidate for a permanent check. AppFactory brings that experience into the next build—with pass/fail checks for problems that can survive an ordinary visual review.

↻ 3 compliance review rounds
Source-level checks, running-simulator checks, and a final review against live App Store Connect state. Failures return to the work that needs fixing.
◇ 10 QA test categories
From purchases and translations to accessibility, offline behavior, and iPhone/iPad parity. A 30–50-item human walkthrough remains a final manual gate.
↗ Purpose-built prompts & critics
13 specialists with scoped tools and budget limits. Separate visual-craft and domain-expert reviews ask whether the app feels finished—and right for the people using it.
≡ 4 enforcement hooks
Directory isolation, post-build checks, submission blocking, and repair-budget controls put boundaries around what the automation can do.

What the checks actually catch52 checks, 3 review rounds, and 10 QA categories.+

The suite targets known rejection and clone-risk failure modes. 51 checks share a combined pass/fail runner; free-trial territory coverage runs at its own gate.

  • Purchases that exist in the account but are missing from the app’s submission.
  • A paywall pointing at the wrong purchase package.
  • Repeated copies of one screenshot presented as a complete screenshot set.
  • Untranslated paywalls, launch-crashing dependencies, or trial availability that disagrees with the listing.

Review & refinement passes

QA covers edge-case inputs, saved state, localization, subscription flow, accessibility, store-listing review, network failure, app lifecycle, visual regression, and iPhone/iPad parity. The visual critic allows up to 3 refinement rounds; the domain expert reviews the app from its intended user’s perspective.

The specialists & launch coverage13 agents. 13 procedure packs. Localization built into the workflow.+

Approximately 2,300 lines of specialist prompts and 1,400 lines of procedure packs support focused roles, each with a tool allowlist and a turn budget.
