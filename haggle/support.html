Common Questions

How do I start a practice session?
Tap any scenario from the Scenarios tab, choose your difficulty level, and start negotiating with the AI opponent.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, find Haggle Pro, and tap Cancel Subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?
Go to the subscription screen in the app and tap "Restore Purchases" at the bottom.

How do I delete my data?
Go to Settings in the app, scroll to the bottom, and tap "Clear All Data."
