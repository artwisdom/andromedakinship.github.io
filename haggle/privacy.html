Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Andromeda Kinship ("we", "us", "our") operates the Haggle - AI Negotiation Coach mobile application ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains what the App keeps on your device, what it sends elsewhere and why, and how you can remove it.

The App has no accounts and no advertising, and we don't sell your data or use it to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

What Stays on Your Device

Your session history, scores, game plans, drill results, coach conversation, whether you've opened the Playbook, settings and setup answers (what you're negotiating, when, and how confident you feel) are stored on your device. We don't keep a copy on our own servers. Reminders are scheduled on your device too.

What Is Sent to OpenAI

The App's AI features need your consent before they run, and they send data to OpenAI, our AI provider, to generate responses and feedback. Depending on what you use, that data is:

  • Your practice conversation messages and the scenario you selected
  • What you type or pick in Prep Mode, the game plan made from it when you rehearse it, and what you type into drills, the Playbook and custom scenarios
  • Your conversation with the AI coach, along with your practice stats: scores, streaks, session counts, milestones, plan tier and session titles
  • On Haggle Pro, your recent session titles and skill scores after a session, to write a short note on your patterns that the coach can read
  • If you use the AI coach and agreed to it, your setup answers

We add nothing that identifies you. If you type a name, an employer or anything else personal, it is sent as you wrote it, so please leave out anything you don't want to share. Haggle gives practice and general coaching, not legal or financial advice, so it never needs account numbers, medical records or anything like them.

OpenAI may keep this data for up to 30 days to monitor for abuse, or longer where the law requires it or to protect its services or others from harm, and handles it according to its data usage policies.

Our Server

AI requests pass through a small server of ours, hosted on Cloudflare, which keeps our OpenAI key out of the App. It uses an anonymous app ID to limit usage and to check whether you have Haggle Pro, and it doesn't pass that ID on to OpenAI. It doesn't store your messages. Cloudflare handles connection data such as your IP address in order to deliver each request.

Microphone and Speech Recognition

The App listens only after you tap the mic in a practice conversation. Apple's speech recognition turns your words into text, on your device where it can and otherwise on Apple's servers, under Apple's privacy policy. The App keeps no recording. You can edit the text before sending it, and only the text is sent to OpenAI. Your counterpart's spoken replies are generated on your device.

Subscriptions and Purchase Data

Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details.

We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app ID, your purchase history and subscription status, and your device's locale and currency. We also record there which screen showed the upgrade page, how many times it has been shown, and which screen led to a purchase, so we can tell which parts of the App are useful.

When the App is installed, Apple's AdServices framework can provide a token, which RevenueCat attaches to the same anonymous record. If an Apple Search Ads ad led to the install, the token shows which campaign and keyword it was. This lets us measure whether our ads work. It isn't used to track you on other apps or websites.

RevenueCat keeps this anonymous record until you ask us to delete it, or until we stop using its service.

Apple also gives us aggregate sales, usage and crash reports.

Our Service Providers

OpenAI, RevenueCat and Cloudflare each handle this data for us as service providers, and their published data processing terms bind them to protect it with appropriate technical and organizational security measures, which gives it protection equal to what this Policy describes.

Your Choices

  • Delete your data: Clear All Data in Settings deletes your sessions, scores and progress, game plans, custom scenarios, coach conversation and notes, drill results, setup answers, settings and AI consent from your device. It keeps only what holds nothing you wrote: the counters behind your free limits (today's free session, today's free coach messages and the free game plan), your trial reminder dates, and RevenueCat's anonymous app ID. It doesn't change anything held by RevenueCat or Apple.
  • Withdraw consent: You can revoke AI data consent in Settings at any time. AI features then stop until you agree again.
  • Microphone: You can turn off microphone and speech recognition access in iOS Settings. Typing works without them.
  • Other requests: For anything held by RevenueCat, including deletion, or any other privacy request, email us at the address below. Because the App has no accounts, we may need your help to identify your record.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy
