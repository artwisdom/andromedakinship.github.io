The game

You are a small alien curled into a glowing orb, and your ship lies crashed in Alaska. Roll home through eight real-world regions, each with its own weather, landmarks and wildlife. Pass under a sea arch, cross a lagoon on a boardwalk, and outrun a suspension bridge as it falls away behind you. Bears rear up and charge, wolves prowl the snow, snakes coil and strike, and elk and caribou bolt alongside. Every charge is telegraphed, so you can read it and swerve. The journey ends under the aurora, where your UFO wakes and carries you home.

  8. Alaskan Wilderness, The Full Journey

How it plays

Momentum carries through every turn. Gold orbs bank up to three boosts that fire when you choose, three in a row charge a SUPER BOOST that smashes logs and boulders, and the brake is real. Play on Easy, Medium or Hard, steering with the on-screen stick or by tilting the phone.

One course for the whole world

The Daily Sprint is a new 1.2 km course every day, the same for every player, with streaks and a Game Center leaderboard that resets when the course changes.

Made with care

Built in Godot 4. Every animal is an original procedural model with its own skeleton and animation, built in the engine. The music is original. The App Store listing is in 25 locales, and the purchase screens follow the device language in each of them.

Fair play

No ads. No tracking. No energy timers, no subscription, no account, and it plays offline. Here is exactly what is free and what the one unlock adds.

Free to play

Regions 1–4 and the Daily Sprint

  • San Diego Coastal Woods, Pacific Coast Highway, Redwood Forest and Oregon Mountains, at Easy, Medium and Hard: half the road home, to a finish gate at the Washington border
  • The leaderboards for regions 1–4, and 12 medals to chase
  • A new Daily Sprint every day, in whichever of the eight regions it runs, with streaks and a Game Center leaderboard
  • Every crystal skin and all five achievements, earned by playing

One optional unlock · $4.99 US

The Full Journey

  • Regions 5–8, from the Washington Rainforest to the Alaskan Wilderness
  • The homecoming and UFO finale
  • Starting at any region you have reached
  • Every past Daily Sprint, to replay unranked
  • The full-journey leaderboards, the leaderboards for regions 5–8 and 12 more medals
  • The HOMECOMING skin
