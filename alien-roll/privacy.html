Privacy Policy — Alien Roll: Rolling Ball Race

Last Updated: October 9, 2026 | Effective Date: October 9, 2026

Andromeda Kinship LLC ("Andromeda Kinship," "we," "our," or "us"), of Fall River, Massachusetts, USA, publishes the iOS game Alien Roll: Rolling Ball Race (the "Game"). In the Game you roll home as an alien curled into a glowing orb, racing through eight real-world regions from San Diego to Alaska. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Game does and does not handle, and the choices available to you.

The short version: We do not collect any data. Alien Roll has no account, no login, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Your progress and settings are stored only on your device. Purchases are processed by Apple, and we receive no payment information and no personal information. Game Center is optional and is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The Game's App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected."

1. No Account, No Sign-Up

Alien Roll does not require an account, registration, or login of any kind. We do not create a user profile for you, and we never ask for your name, email address, or any password. You can play the whole free game, and use a purchase, without ever giving us anything.

2. Information We Collect

None. We, the developer, do not collect, receive, or store personal information or usage data from the Game. In particular:

  • We do not run any server for the Game. The Game does not send your progress, your scores, or anything else to us.
  • The Game contains no advertising, analytics, attribution, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs or other third-party code that collects data.
  • The Game does not use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not ask for permission to track you. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • The Game does not ask for access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or health data.

2.1 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • Apple Account details
  • Payment card, bank, or other financial account information
  • Contacts, photos, or other content on your device
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Advertising identifiers or device identifiers
  • Gameplay, usage, crash, or diagnostic data
  • Health or fitness data

3. Data Stored on Your Device

To work, the Game saves a small amount of information locally on your device. None of it is sent to us.

  • Progress: the regions you have reached, your best times and medals, your Daily Sprint results and streaks, and the skins you have earned.
  • Settings: your control choice (on-screen stick or tilt), difficulty, music, sound, haptics, and similar preferences.
  • Unsent leaderboard times: if you use Game Center and a time cannot be posted right away (for example, while you are offline), the Game keeps it on the device and tries again later.
  • Purchase record: after Apple confirms your purchase of The Full Journey, the Game keeps a small record of that confirmation in your device's Keychain, so the unlock works even when you are offline. This record contains no personal information. It stays on this device only, and it is never synced or sent to us. iOS may keep it after the Game is deleted, so a reinstall unlocks at once.

Deleting the Game removes its saved progress and settings from your device. If you back up your device (for example, with iCloud Backup), those backups may include the Game's saved progress. Apple handles device backups under your own backup settings. We do not have a copy of your progress, so we cannot restore it for you.

3.1 Tilt Steering

If you choose tilt steering, the Game reads your device's motion sensor in real time to steer. These readings are used only on your device while you play. They are never stored or sent anywhere.

3.2 Age Range Check (Where the Law Requires It)

On recent versions of iOS, in places whose law requires apps to ask, the Game may ask iOS for your age range using Apple's Declared Age Range feature. You decide whether to share it, and Apple shows its own prompt. If you share it, the Game receives only an age range (never your date of birth). The range is kept in memory for that session only. It is never saved, never sent to us, and does not change how the Game plays.

4. Purchases (Processed by Apple)

Alien Roll is free to download. It offers one optional in-app purchase, The Full Journey, a one-time, non-consumable purchase made through Apple's App Store (StoreKit).

  • Apple processes every purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple and billed to your Apple Account. We never receive or store your payment card, bank details, name, email address, or Apple Account details.
  • Apple gives the Game, on your device, a signed confirmation of your purchase so the Game can unlock it and restore it. That confirmation is checked on your device and is not sent to us.
  • Apple provides us only with aggregate sales and download reports, which do not identify you.
  • Family Sharing and Ask to Buy are managed by Apple. If you share the purchase with your family, Apple handles that sharing.

Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Game Center (Optional)

Alien Roll uses Apple's Game Center for leaderboards, including the Daily Sprint board. Game Center is optional: the Game is fully playable without it.

  • If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, the Game posts your finish times to Apple's Game Center leaderboards and loads leaderboard entries to show you. Other players may see your times with your Game Center nickname, according to your Game Center settings.
  • Sign-in, your Game Center profile, friends, and leaderboards are provided and run by Apple. The Game does not send any Game Center information to us, and we do not receive your Apple Account details.
  • If you are not signed in, your times stay on your device and are not posted.
  • You can sign out of Game Center, or change who can see your profile, in your device's Settings under Game Center. Parents can restrict Game Center with Screen Time.

Game Center is governed by Apple's privacy policy. See Game Center & Privacy.

6. Other Apple Features

  • Ratings prompt: the Game may occasionally show Apple's standard prompt to rate the Game. Ratings and reviews are handled by Apple. We see only what Apple publishes on the App Store.
  • Apple analytics you control: if you have turned on Share With App Developers in your device's Settings (Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with developers in aggregated or de-identified form. This is Apple's feature, controlled by you. The Game itself sends nothing.

8. This Website

When you tap the Privacy or Terms link in the Game, or visit our support page, your browser opens pages on andromedakinship.com. These policy pages use no cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may process standard server logs (such as your IP address) as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

9. Data Is Not Sold or Shared

We do not sell or share your personal information, because we do not collect it. We do not use your information for targeted advertising, profiling, or any automated decision-making.

10. Children and Age

Alien Roll is rated 9+ on the App Store and is made for a general audience, including children. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children, including children under 13. The Game has no ads, no chat or messaging, no user-generated content, and no links to social media. Purchases by children can be managed with Apple's Ask to Buy and Screen Time, and Game Center can be restricted with Screen Time. If you are a parent or guardian with a question, please contact us (Section 13).

11. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR (EU and UK) or the CCPA (California) give you rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal data, and to object to certain uses of it. Because we do not collect or hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete. You remain in control of what is on your device:

  • Delete the Game to remove its saved progress and settings.
  • Manage Game Center in your device's Settings.
  • For data Apple holds about your purchases or Game Center, use the privacy tools Apple provides and see Apple's Privacy Policy.

You are always welcome to contact us with a privacy question or request.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we change how the Game handles information, we will update this page and the "Last Updated" date above before the change takes effect. If a future version ever collects data, this policy and the Game's App Store privacy label will say so first.
