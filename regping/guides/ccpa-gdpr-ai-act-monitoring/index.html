Tracking CCPA, GDPR
& the EU AI Act

Privacy and AI regulation is moving fast on two continents at once — and unlike a single agency beat, the sources are scattered across state regulators, attorneys general, and EU bodies. Some of the most consequential moves don't have an official feed at all.

The sources, and the gaps between them

No single feed covers privacy and AI law, so effective monitoring combines primary regulators with a trusted aggregator to catch what would otherwise slip through:

  • CPPA — the California Privacy Protection Agency, for enforcement, advisories, and rulemaking under the CCPA/CPRA.
  • California AG — a broad news feed that you'll want to filter at ingest to privacy and AI items (CCPA, CPRA, data, AI, algorithms, biometrics, breaches).
  • EDPB — the European Data Protection Board, for GDPR enforcement and guidance.
  • A specialist aggregator — to catch multi-state AG actions and EU AI Act developments that have no primary feed of their own.

That last category is the trap: if you only watch official agency pages, you'll miss the multi-state actions and the AI Act steps that surface first through specialist coverage.

Scope by jurisdiction and topic

Privacy and AI teams rarely care about everything equally. A US-only product doesn't need every EDPB guidance note; an EU-focused team doesn't need every California breach notice. The fix is filtering across a few axes:

  • Jurisdiction — US-CA, EU, or US-multistate.
  • Action type — enforcement, rulemaking, guidance, consultation, legislation, or commentary.
  • Topic — AI, privacy, GDPR, CCPA, or biometric.
  • Severity — so routine commentary never pings the channel if you only want enforcement.

De-duplication matters more here than anywhere

Because you're combining primary sources with an aggregator, the same development will often show up twice — once on the agency page, once in the aggregator's coverage. Without de-duplication, your channel double-posts and your team learns to tune it out. Suppressing near-duplicates is what keeps a multi-source feed trustworthy.

FAQ

How do I monitor CCPA enforcement?
Watch the CPPA for enforcement, advisories, and rulemaking, and the California AG's news feed filtered to privacy and AI items, then route new items to where your team works.
Where do I track the EU AI Act and GDPR enforcement?
GDPR enforcement and guidance come from the EDPB. EU AI Act developments are more scattered and often surface first through specialist aggregators rather than a single official feed.
Why is this so hard to monitor?
The sources are fragmented across agencies, state AGs, and EU bodies, and some major developments have no primary feed — so you combine official sources with a trusted aggregator and de-duplicate.
