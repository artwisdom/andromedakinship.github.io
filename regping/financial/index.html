Every alert carries a plain-text severity label so your team can triage at a glance:

Financial monitoring FAQ

Which financial regulators does it monitor?
Seven: the SEC, FINRA, the OCC, the FDIC, NYDFS, FinCEN, and the FTC (filtered to financial and fintech matters such as payments, lending, BNPL, crypto, and data brokers).
How do I track SEC and FINRA actions in real time?
Add the bot to your server; it checks each regulator's public feed every 30 minutes and posts a summarized, severity-tagged alert with a link to the original notice whenever a new action publishes.
Can I filter to only the agencies that apply to me?
Yes — use /filter to scope by agency and set a minimum severity, so each team member sees only the regulators and the urgency level that matter to your business.
Is this legal or compliance advice?
No. RegPing Financial is a monitoring and awareness service. It quotes and links each regulator's own notice and is not a substitute for counsel.
