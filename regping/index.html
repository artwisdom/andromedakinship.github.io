How it works

One shared engine, four verticals. Add a bot, pick a channel, set your filters — and let it run.

01

Add the bot

Install your vertical to your server and point it at a channel — say #regulatory-alerts.

02

Set filters

Use /filter to scope by agency, state, topic, and minimum severity. Your team, your noise floor.

03

It watches

Every 30 minutes it checks the official sources, de-duplicates, and summarizes new actions in plain language.

04

You get pinged

A clean embed lands in your channel — headline, synopsis, severity, and a link to the regulator's own notice.

RegPing republishes facts from public regulators on a quote-and-link basis, and every alert links to the primary source. RegPing is a monitoring and awareness service — not legal advice, and not a substitute for counsel or an enterprise compliance platform. Billing is per Discord server and covers your whole team.
