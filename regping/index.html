Frequently asked questions

What is RegPing?
RegPing is a suite of regulatory-monitoring bots for Discord. Each bot watches a set of public regulator sources, summarizes new regulatory actions in plain language, and posts them as a clean embed into the channel you choose.
How does RegPing deliver regulatory alerts?
Every 30 minutes each bot checks its official sources, de-duplicates against what it has already seen, summarizes new items, and posts an embed — headline, synopsis, severity label, and a link to the regulator's own notice — into your designated Discord channel.
How much does RegPing cost?
Pricing is per Discord server and covers your whole team: RegPing FDA is $9.99/month, RegPing Cannabis is $14.99/month, and RegPing Financial and RegPing AI & Privacy are $19.99/month each.
Is RegPing legal advice?
No. RegPing is a monitoring and awareness service. It republishes facts from public regulators on a quote-and-link basis and links each alert to the primary source. It is not legal advice and not a substitute for counsel.
Can I filter which alerts I receive?
Yes. Use the /filter slash command to scope each feed by agency or jurisdiction, by topic, and by a minimum severity level, so your team only sees what is relevant to your business.
