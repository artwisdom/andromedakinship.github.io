States covered

RegPing Cannabis tracks the agencies that actually publish the rules in each market:

California — DCCDepartment of Cannabis Control.
Oregon — OLCCLiquor & Cannabis Commission (industry bulletins).
Massachusetts — CCCCannabis Control Commission (official RSS).
Illinois — IDFPRDept. of Financial & Professional Regulation.
New Jersey — CRCCannabis Regulatory Commission.
New York — OCMOffice of Cannabis Management.

Washington (LCB) is coming soon. Massachusetts is monitored via the CCC's official RSS feed; the remaining states are tracked by polite monitoring of each regulator's public press-release, bulletin, newsletter, and rulemaking pages.

Rule citations, not just headlines

Each new item — a press release, bulletin, newsletter, enforcement action, or rulemaking notice — is summarized and posted as an embed, with rule citations (for example, an OLCC industry-bulletin number) included where the regulator provides them. Every alert links to the regulator's own page so your compliance lead can read the primary source in one click.

Who it's for

Compliance staff, license holders, and operations managers at dispensaries and multi-state operators (MSOs) who need to stay aware of rule changes in the specific states they operate in — lean teams tracking a fragmented, fast-moving regulatory map without an enterprise compliance suite.

RegPing Cannabis is an awareness tool, not legal advice — and it is not a seed-to-sale traceability system or a substitute for state-mandated platforms like METRC.
