RegPing Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 18, 2026 Contact: michael@complianceghost.com

1. Who We Are

RegPing is a suite of Discord application bots ("RegPing," "we," "us," or "our") that monitor public regulatory feeds — including FDA recalls, US financial-regulator enforcement actions, US state cannabis rules, and AI/privacy legislation — and deliver filtered alert embeds into subscribing Discord servers. RegPing is operated by an individual developer. Questions about this policy may be sent to michael@complianceghost.com.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all data processed by the RegPing bots in connection with their operation on Discord. It does not govern Discord's own data practices; please review Discord's Privacy Policy separately.

3. What We Collect and Why

RegPing collects and stores only the minimum data necessary to deliver its alert service. Specifically, we store:

Data Element Purpose
Discord guild (server) ID Identifies the subscribing server so alerts can be routed correctly
Discord channel ID Identifies the channel within a server where alert embeds are posted
Filter preferences Per-server settings chosen by a server administrator, such as selected states, regulatory agencies, or severity levels, that control which alerts are delivered
Subscription/entitlement status Discord-reported entitlement state, used to determine whether a server has an active paid subscription

We collect this data because it is strictly necessary to provide the service. Without a guild ID, channel ID, and filter configuration, we cannot route alerts to the correct destination.

4. What We Do Not Collect

RegPing explicitly does not collect, process, or store:

  • Message content — RegPing operates exclusively via Discord's HTTP interactions model and maintains no persistent gateway connection to Discord. RegPing is technically incapable of reading any messages posted in your server, including direct messages.
  • User personal information — We do not collect names, email addresses, usernames, IP addresses, or any other information attributable to individual Discord users.
  • Member lists — We do not access or store information about who is a member of any server.
  • Message history — We do not retrieve or store historical messages from any server.
  • Payment or financial information — All billing is handled entirely by Discord through its Premium Apps / Guild Subscriptions system. Payment card numbers, billing addresses, and financial account details never reach RegPing's systems.

RegPing requests only the applications.commands and bot OAuth scopes, with Send Messages and Embed Links permissions. These permissions are sufficient to post alert embeds and respond to slash commands, and are the only permissions RegPing holds.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis (including under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, "GDPR"):

  • Contractual necessity — Processing guild IDs, channel IDs, filter preferences, and entitlement status is necessary to perform the service you have requested by adding RegPing to your server.
  • Legitimate interests — Where applicable, we rely on our legitimate interests in operating and improving a reliable regulatory-alert service, balanced against the minimal privacy impact given that we process no personal data about individual users.

6. How Data Is Stored

All RegPing operational data is stored in Cloudflare Workers KV, a globally distributed key-value store operated by Cloudflare, Inc. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Cloudflare operates data centers globally; data may be stored or replicated in multiple regions as part of normal Cloudflare KV operation. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy for details on Cloudflare's data practices.

We do not operate our own database servers or persistent file storage outside of Cloudflare's platform.

7. Retention

RegPing retains server configuration data (guild ID, channel ID, filter preferences, entitlement status) for as long as the bot remains in your server and your subscription remains active or was recently active. If a server removes the RegPing bot or the subscription lapses, the associated configuration data is eligible for deletion within 90 days of the bot's removal or subscription expiry. You may also request earlier deletion by contacting michael@complianceghost.com.

8. Sub-Processors

RegPing relies on the following third-party sub-processors:

Sub-Processor Role Privacy Policy
Cloudflare, Inc. Application hosting and KV data storage cloudflare.com/privacypolicy
Anthropic, PBC Claude API — used to summarize regulatory text before posting anthropic.com/privacy
Discord, Inc. Platform delivery, OAuth, subscription/entitlement management discord.com/privacy

Important note regarding Anthropic: When RegPing uses the Anthropic Claude API to summarize regulatory content, only the publicly available regulatory text published by government agencies is sent to Anthropic. No guild IDs, channel IDs, user data, server data, or filter preferences are ever transmitted to Anthropic.

9. No Sale of Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade any data we hold to third parties for their own marketing or commercial purposes.

10. Your Rights — Server Administrators

Because RegPing does not collect data about individual users, most data-subject rights (access, rectification, erasure) apply at the server-administrator level:

  • Right to access — You may request a copy of all data RegPing holds for your server by contacting michael@complianceghost.com with your server's guild ID.
  • Right to erasure / right to stop processing — You may stop all processing immediately by:
    1. Removing the RegPing bot from your server, which terminates the bot's permissions and triggers deletion of your server's configuration data; or
    2. Using the /unsubscribe command within your server, which removes your server's subscription and configuration. Alternatively, contact michael@complianceghost.com to request manual deletion.
  • Right to rectification — Filter preferences and channel settings can be updated at any time using RegPing's slash commands. Contact michael@complianceghost.com if you need further corrections.
  • Right to object / restrict — You may object to processing by removing the bot or contacting us. We have no automated profiling or decision-making processes.

11. GDPR Considerations

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland:

  • The data controller for your server's configuration data is the RegPing operator (contact: michael@complianceghost.com).
  • You have the rights described in Section 10, plus the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.
  • To the extent any personal data is processed (which is minimal given RegPing stores only server- and channel-level identifiers), we rely on contractual necessity as our primary legal basis (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • If you believe any processing involves personal data, please contact us at michael@complianceghost.com.

12. CCPA Considerations

If you are a California resident, note that:

  • RegPing does not sell personal information.
  • RegPing does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • The data RegPing stores (guild IDs, channel IDs, server-level preferences) does not directly identify natural persons. To the extent any California personal information is implicated, you may request disclosure or deletion by contacting michael@complianceghost.com.

13. Children

RegPing is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 where required by applicable law), and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Compliance with Discord's age requirements is the responsibility of server administrators.

14. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the data we hold, including reliance on Cloudflare's infrastructure security, HTTPS-only communication, and minimal data collection. No system is perfectly secure; by using RegPing you acknowledge that transmissions over the internet carry inherent risk.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced via RegPing's official support server or Discord application description. Continued use of RegPing after the revised effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
