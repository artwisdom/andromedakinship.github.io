Common questions

How do I log a sale?

Tap the “+” button on the Home screen to start a new write-up. Add the piece, any add-ons or care plans, the credit-card application you opened, and your SPIFF — the app computes your take-home pay automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. All of your write-ups and your client book are stored locally on your device. No account or login is required, and the app works fully offline. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. Open the iOS Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions, or go to apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. You can also tap “Manage Subscription” in the app’s Settings screen.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the app’s subscription screen and tap Restore Purchases. Your subscription is tied to your Apple ID and will restore on any device signed in with the same account.

I changed stores — can I keep my client book?

Yes. Your client book and history live on your device, independent of any store system, so they travel with you.
