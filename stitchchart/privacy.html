StitchChart — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026
Effective: July 30, 2026

Who we are

StitchChart is published by Andromeda Kinship LLC (Fall River, Massachusetts, USA). Contact: support@andromedakinship.com.

The short version

StitchChart does not require an account, does not collect personal information, and does not upload the cross-stitch charts you import. Charts and your stitching progress are stored on your device.

What we collect

We collect no personal data. There is no sign-up, no login, and no user profile.

Everything you create in StitchChart — the charts you import, your marked stitches, thread notes, and settings — is stored locally on your device using iOS's standard on-device storage. We do not keep a server-side copy.

The only data that leaves your device is:

  • Purchase records — if you subscribe or restore a purchase, RevenueCat (our subscription management provider) receives a purchase token from Apple's StoreKit in order to verify your subscription status. This token identifies the purchase, not you. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. Apple also processes the payment under its own privacy policy.

Your charts

When you import a chart — a PDF, an .oxs file, a photo from your library, or a photo you take — StitchChart reads it entirely on your device. The file is not uploaded to us or to any third party, and it is not sent to any cloud service for processing. StitchChart does not host, share, distribute, or make available any cross-stitch patterns; you bring charts you already own.

Camera and photo access

If you choose to photograph a paper chart or pick an image from your photo library, iOS will ask your permission. StitchChart uses that access solely to read the chart image you selected, on your device. We do not scan, index, or upload your photo library.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with anyone.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs that profile individual users.
  • We do not upload, host, or distribute the charts you import.

Children

StitchChart is a general-audience craft tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your control and your data

Because your data lives on your device, you control it directly. Deleting a project removes its chart and progress from the device. Deleting the app removes everything StitchChart has stored. There is no account for us to delete, because no account is ever created.

Thread brands

DMC, Anchor, and Madeira are trademarks of their respective owners. StitchChart is an independent app and is not affiliated with, authorized, or endorsed by them. Thread numbers are provided for reference only.

Changes
