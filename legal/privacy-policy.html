Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 13, 2026

Andromeda Kinship ("we," "our," or "us") operates the SellCraft mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Your name and sales industry selection during onboarding.
  • Chat Messages: Messages you send to the AI coaching feature (Chiron).
  • Goals and Progress: Sales goals, objection practice results, and training progress you create within the App.
  • Profile Photo: If you choose to add a profile photo using your device's camera or photo library.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: App interaction data such as features used, session duration, and screens viewed.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and app version.
  • Purchase Data: Subscription status and transaction identifiers (processed through Apple and RevenueCat).
  • Crash Data: Diagnostic information including crash logs to improve app stability.

2. Third-Party AI Service — Data Sharing Disclosure

Important: SellCraft uses OpenAI's API to power the AI coaching feature (Chiron). When you use the AI chat feature, the following data is transmitted to OpenAI:

  • Your chat messages and conversation history (up to the last 10 messages)
  • Your name and selected sales industry
  • Your app statistics (level, streak, readiness score, goals summary, and daily/weekly performance data)

This data is sent securely via encrypted HTTPS connections to OpenAI's servers to generate personalized AI coaching responses. Before using the AI chat feature for the first time, you will be asked to consent to this data sharing through an in-app disclosure.

OpenAI's data handling:

  • Data sent via the OpenAI API is not used to train OpenAI's models per their API data usage policy.
  • OpenAI retains API data for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring, after which it is deleted.
  • For more details, see OpenAI's API Data Usage Policy.

If you decline the AI data sharing consent, you can still use all other features of SellCraft. The AI chat feature will provide template-based responses that do not transmit data to third parties.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's features and functionality
  • Deliver personalized AI coaching responses through OpenAI's API
  • Track your sales training progress, goals, and performance
  • Send push notifications related to your training goals and reminders
  • Process subscription payments through Apple's In-App Purchase system
  • Improve the App's performance and fix bugs

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • OpenAI: AI coaching responses (receives chat messages and user context as described in Section 2)
  • RevenueCat: Subscription management (receives anonymous user identifiers and purchase data)
  • Apple: In-App Purchases, push notifications, and crash reporting
  • Expo: App build infrastructure and over-the-air updates

Each third-party service processes data in accordance with their own privacy policies and provides the same or equivalent level of data protection.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your personal data (name, goals, progress, chat history) is stored locally on your device using encrypted storage. We do not maintain user accounts or store personal data on our servers. AI chat messages are transmitted to OpenAI for processing and are not permanently stored by us.

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including HTTPS encryption for all data transmissions.

6. Data Retention

  • Local Data: Stored on your device until you delete the app or reset app data.
  • OpenAI Data: Retained by OpenAI for up to 30 days per their API data usage policy, then deleted.
  • RevenueCat Data: Purchase records retained as required for subscription management.

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • AI Data Consent: You can choose to decline AI data sharing. Template-based coaching will still be available.
  • Data Deletion: You can delete all local app data through the "Reset All Data" option in the App's settings.
  • Notifications: You can disable push notifications through your device settings.
  • Subscription: You can cancel your subscription at any time through your Apple ID account settings.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

9. Subscriptions

SellCraft offers auto-renewable subscriptions: SellCraft Pro Monthly at $19.99 per month and SellCraft Pro Yearly at $169.99 per year. Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription automatically renews unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID account settings.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
