Glidepath: Taper Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026
Effective: August 16, 2026

Who we are

Glidepath: Taper Tracker is published by Andromeda Kinship LLC (Fall River, Massachusetts, USA). Contact: support@andromedakinship.com.

What we collect

Glidepath stores your data locally on your device using iOS's standard on-device storage (AsyncStorage). This includes any taper plan you create, dose entries, and symptom check-ins you log — all of it health-related information you choose to enter. We do not maintain a server-side copy of your entries, settings, or any data you input, and Glidepath has no account system, no login, and no cloud sync.

The only data that leaves your device is:

  • Purchase records — when you subscribe or restore purchases, RevenueCat (our subscription management provider) receives a purchase token from Apple's StoreKit. RevenueCat uses this to verify your subscription status. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we do with it

Locally-stored data stays on your device and is used solely to render your taper schedule, log entries, symptom charts, and the Stability Window comparison, and to persist that information between sessions. Purchase data is used only to gate premium features behind your active subscription. If you choose to export your data (for example, to share a report with your prescriber), that export is created and saved by you — we never receive a copy.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or health data with any third party for marketing purposes.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not show you ads.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs that profile individual users.
  • We do not use AI or send your dose or symptom data to any third-party AI service.
  • We do not require an account or any personal identifier to use the app.

Crisis resources

Glidepath displays crisis resources (the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, Crisis Text Line, and findahelpline.com) directly in the app, always available from Settings and Home, at no cost and behind no paywall. Tapping a crisis resource opens your device's phone, messaging, or browser app directly — Glidepath does not log, transmit, or store the fact that you viewed or used these resources.

Your rights

Because Glidepath stores your data locally, you can delete all of it at any time by uninstalling the app, or by using the "Clear data" option in Settings. The app has no account system and no login, so there is no server-side profile to delete.

For purchase data held by RevenueCat or Apple, refer to their respective privacy policies for deletion and access rights.

Children

Glidepath is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If we materially change how data flows in Glidepath, we'll update this page and note the change in the app's release notes. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
